The Automation Stack

Everything That Should Happen Automatically

We build a complete automation infrastructure that handles lead response, nurturing, booking, confirmation, and post-job follow-up — so nothing falls through the cracks.

1

AI Speed-to-Lead (Under 60 Seconds)

The second a lead comes in — from your website, Facebook, Google, or anywhere else — our AI system initiates contact within 60 seconds via text and phone. Research shows response time is the #1 factor in whether a lead converts. We make sure you're always first.

2

Automated Qualification

Our system asks the right questions to qualify the lead — project type, timeline, budget range, and homeownership status — before they ever hit your calendar. Tire-kickers get filtered out automatically.

3

Self-Booking Calendar

Qualified leads are presented with your availability and book themselves directly into your calendar. No phone tag, no back-and-forth texting. They pick a time, and it's confirmed instantly.

4

Smart Confirmation & Reminders

Automated text and email reminders at 24 hours, 2 hours, and 30 minutes before their appointment. Each touchpoint confirms the visit and gives them easy options to reschedule if needed — reducing no-shows to under 5%.

5

Long-Term Nurture Sequences

Leads that don't book immediately enter a multi-touch follow-up sequence — SMS, email, and voicemail drops over 30, 60, and 90 days. When they're ready, you're the contractor they remember.

6

Post-Job Review Automation

After every completed job, our system sends a personalized review request. Happy customers get a direct link to leave a Google review. This builds your online reputation automatically and drives more organic leads.

Stop Doing Everything Manually

Let the system handle the busywork so you can focus on what you do best — closing premium jobs.
