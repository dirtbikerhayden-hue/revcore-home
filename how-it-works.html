The Process

From Zero to Booked Appointments
In 14 Days

We handle everything — strategy, creative, tech, automation. You show up, present your premium work, and close. Here's exactly how it works.

1

Discovery & Strategy Session (Day 1)

We start with a deep dive into your business: your trade, your ideal customer, your average job ticket, your close rate, and your service area. We map out the high-income zip codes in your market and identify exactly where the premium homeowners are. By the end of this call, you'll have a complete growth roadmap.

2

System Build & Creative (Days 2-10)

Our team builds everything: ad creative tailored to your trade, conversion-optimized landing pages, your booking system, follow-up automations, and qualification flows. We write the copy, design the assets, and configure every automation so it's ready to go live.

3

Launch & Go Live (Day 14)

Campaigns go live. Ads start running to your ideal homeowners. Our speed-to-lead system activates — every inquiry gets an instant response. Qualified leads start booking into your calendar. You'll typically see your first appointments within the first few days of launch.

4

Optimize & Scale (Ongoing)

This is where most agencies disappear. We don't. Our team optimizes your campaigns daily — adjusting targeting, testing new creative, refining qualification criteria, and scaling what works. You get a dedicated account manager and regular performance reports showing exactly where every dollar went and what it produced.
